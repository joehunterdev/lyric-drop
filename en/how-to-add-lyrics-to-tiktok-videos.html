How to Add Lyrics to TikTok Videos (Without a Video Editor)

Adding lyrics to short-form videos used to mean downloading CapCut, learning a timeline, manually typing line by line, syncing each one frame-by-frame, and exporting — only to realize the timing is off on the chorus. Then doing it all again.

Lyric Drop replaces that entire process with one browser tab.

What you need

  • Your video file (MP4, MOV, WebM, AVI supported)
  • The full lyrics — paste the entire sheet at once
  • A browser — no install, no account

Step 1 — Upload your video

Open Lyric Drop and drag your video into the upload zone. The player loads instantly in the browser — no uploading to a server, no waiting. Everything runs locally.

Step 2 — Paste your full lyrics

Don't pre-segment them. Don't count syllables. Just paste the entire lyric sheet into the editor — verse, chorus, bridge, everything. Lyric Drop handles the distribution.

Step 3 — Set your start and end points

Use the visual timeline to drag the lyric block to where the song starts and ends in your video. The app automatically spreads the lyrics evenly across that duration. You can fine-tune individual segments by dragging them on the timeline.

Step 4 — Preview in real time

Hit play. You'll see the lyrics overlaid on your video exactly as they'll appear in the exported file — synced, styled, and ready. Adjust timing by dragging. No guesswork.

Step 5 — Export

Click Export and download your finished video with burned-in lyrics. Upload it directly to TikTok, Instagram Reels, or YouTube Shorts.

Why not just use TikTok's built-in captions?

TikTok's auto-captions are transcription-based — they generate from speech audio, which works poorly for music. They also can't sync to song timing, don't support styled lyric layouts, and can't handle overlapping or rhythmic line breaks the way a lyric video needs.

Lyric Drop gives you full control: timing, style, and a finished video file you own.
